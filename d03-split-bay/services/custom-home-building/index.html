FAQs

Custom Home Building Services FAQ

Addressing your custom home building questions and concerns.

Where does building a custom home actually start?

With the lot, not the floor plan. Zoning, setbacks, grade, drainage, utility availability and — outside city limits — well and septic capacity all constrain what can be built before a single room is designed. Designing first and testing the lot afterwards is how people end up paying twice for drawings.

How long does a custom home take?

Considerably longer than the construction alone, because design, engineering and plan review all precede it and none of them is instant. Review timelines differ substantially between a large city department, a small town office and a county one. We give you a programme that includes those windows rather than a build duration that quietly assumes they take no time.

Do you build on hillside or rural lots?

Yes. Hillside work in places like Fountain Hills, Cave Creek and Paradise Valley brings its own provisions on cut and fill, height and drainage, and access frequently limits what equipment and deliveries can physically reach a site. Rural lots bring well and septic questions that have to be settled before a design is worth drawing.

Can I make changes once building has started?

Yes, and most people do. What we ask is that changes are agreed in writing with their cost and schedule effect stated before the work happens, not after. Verbal changes on a busy site are how disputes start, and they are entirely avoidable.

Do you manage the trades yourself?

Yes — the trades are self-managed rather than handed to a separate project manager who is not answerable for the result. On a custom home the coordination between trades is where the quality actually lives, and it is not something to subcontract away.
