Three directions for the new site

Each card below is a complete, working homepage — not a picture. Hover a preview and click once to scroll and interact with it, or open it full screen. 01 and 02 are built in the layered, dimensional style — cream hero, angular shapes behind the photography, floating cards, soft shadows. 03 is the flat brutalist one, kept as it was.

How to use this page. Start with the three colour dots at the top — they change the accent on all three designs at once, live, so you can settle the colour argument by eye instead of by description. Then use Desktop/Mobile to check how each one reshapes on a phone. Copy is placeholder throughout, and the photography is free stock we can swap for real Quest jobsite photos later. Note: 03 still runs its own slightly hotter accent values, since it was left untouched — say the word and I’ll match them up.
