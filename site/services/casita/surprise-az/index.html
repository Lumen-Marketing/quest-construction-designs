What is different about Surprise

Nearly the whole city is post-1998 and consistent: slab-on-grade, engineered trusses, stucco over frame. Placing a second building is rarely the problem. Making it look like part of the original is, because a twenty-year-old stucco elevation and twenty-year-old roof tile do not match anything currently on a shelf. Tile is sourced and stucco is matched on the wall before either is ordered in quantity.

Inside Sun City Grand and the other age-restricted communities there are architectural rules and contractor access hours to work to, and a casita is often specifically a caregiver or family suite. Those rules get read before we quote, because a gate that closes at four in the afternoon is a fact about the schedule, not a detail.
