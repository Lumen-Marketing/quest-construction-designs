— Roofing in Wickenburg, AZ

What is different about Wickenburg

Barns, shops and outbuildings on acreage carry metal roofing far more often than tile, and metal is its own trade — panel selection, fastener pattern, thermal movement and flashing at every transition. We specify it as metal rather than treating it as a cheap alternative to a shingle roof.

In the historic downtown there is genuine old masonry and adobe, where a roof has to work with a wall that needs to breathe. An impermeable detail against an adobe parapet traps moisture in the wall, and that shows up years later as failed plaster rather than as a leak.
