Building in Goodyear Meadows

Goodyear sits on the west side of the valley, and the housing here is newer than most of the East Valley. A large share of what we do is finishing what a production builder left undone: outdoor living space, extended patios, garage conversions, casitas and the storage a family discovers it needs two years after moving in.

Parts of the West Valley have soils that move with moisture, so foundations, flatwork and drainage deserve more attention here than a homeowner might expect on a newer house. We would rather specify properly at the outset than return to a cracked patio in two summers.

Summer heat governs the schedule more here than almost anywhere else we work. Concrete pours, roofing and exterior coatings get planned around the temperature rather than fought against it, which sometimes means an early start and sometimes means waiting a fortnight.
