Service Areas Across Arizona

Quest Construction builds in 11 Arizona cities. Each one permits differently and each one has its own housing stock — the pages below say how.

— Eleven cities

Where Quest Builds

One crew, one licence, one phone number across the East Valley, Phoenix and Pinal County.

Mesa, AZ

Mesa runs its own Development Services department, so a permit here is a City of Mesa submittal rather than a county one.

Building in Mesa ↗

Tempe, AZ

Tempe is landlocked and effectively built out, so almost everything we do here is infill, remodel or addition rather than new ground.

Building in Tempe ↗

Queen Creek, AZ

Queen Creek has grown quickly, and that shows in what we are asked to build.

Building in Queen Creek ↗

Scottsdale, AZ

Scottsdale expects a higher standard of documentation than most valley cities, and its design review is genuinely enforced.

Building in Scottsdale ↗

Gilbert, AZ

Gilbert is largely master-planned, and a great deal of its housing went up through the 1990s and 2000s.

Building in Gilbert ↗

Chandler, AZ

Chandler spans a wide range of vintages, from established neighbourhoods near the historic centre to newer development further south.

Building in Chandler ↗

Paradise Valley, AZ

Paradise Valley is a town rather than part of Phoenix, and it governs building on its own terms.

Building in Paradise Valley ↗

Phoenix, AZ

Phoenix covers the widest range of housing in the state, from 1920s bungalows near the centre to homes finished last year.

Building in Phoenix ↗

Goodyear Meadows, AZ

Goodyear sits on the west side of the valley, and the housing here is newer than most of the East Valley.

Building in Goodyear Meadows ↗

Camelback East Village, AZ

Camelback East Village is one of the City of Phoenix's urban villages rather than a separate municipality, so work here permits through Phoenix.

Building in Camelback East Village ↗

Florence, AZ

Florence sits in Pinal County rather than Maricopa, which means a different permitting jurisdiction from the rest of our service area.

Building in Florence ↗
