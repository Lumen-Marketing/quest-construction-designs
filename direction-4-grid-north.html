A contractor who answers the phone.

Almost nothing goes wrong on a build because someone cut a stud short. It goes wrong in the three weeks nobody answered a question, while the tile sat on a pallet and the schedule quietly slid a month.

Quest is organised against exactly that. One project manager owns the job from the first walkthrough to the final punch list, and the three trades that set your dates — concrete, framing, finish carpentry — are our own crews rather than a sub we are queuing behind.

What you get is an itemised fixed bid, a written schedule every Friday, and a telephone number that belongs to a person instead of an office.
